# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Modern React / Next.js with Tailwind CSS (clean, fast, responsive RTL-first UI with TypeScript)

## Users

- **Primary Users**: Egyptian pilgrims and families in Assiut, Upper Egypt, and nationwide seeking trustworthy, officially licensed Hajj & Umrah packages.
- **Secondary Users**: Holidaymakers and domestic travelers booking Red Sea beach vacations (Hurghada, Sharm El Sheikh, Dahab), cultural Nile trips, international tours, or airline tickets.
- **Situation & Job**: Planning a significant emotional and financial travel investment. They need to verify the agency's legitimacy, compare package itineraries and inclusions (hotels, distances to Haram, flights, meals), and connect instantly with an agent via WhatsApp or phone call to complete reservation details.

## Product Purpose

A high-trust, conversion-focused digital presence for **إيتاس للسياحة - فرع أسيوط** (*Ittas Tours*). It establishes immediate credibility through official accreditation, presents clear package itineraries with transparent inclusions, and drives direct inquiries through high-touch communication channels (WhatsApp and Phone).

## Positioning

A premier, 48-year accredited tourism institution (Category "A" License No. 167 from the Egyptian Ministry of Tourism since 1976) that combines decades of pilgrim care with modern, personalized travel coordination right in the heart of Assiut.

## Operating Context

- High mobile usage (>80% of visits on mobile smartphones across Upper Egypt).
- Right-to-Left (RTL) Arabic as the primary language with English accessibility.
- Direct conversational commerce: users prefer one-on-one WhatsApp consultation and hotline calls over automated payment gateways for high-ticket travel packages.
- Seasonal travel cycles: peak demand around Umrah seasons (Rajab, Sha'ban, Ramadan, Mawlid), Hajj, and summer holiday breaks.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Core Capabilities**:
  - Flagship showcase for Umrah & Hajj packages (flight schedules, 5-star & economic tiers, distance to Haram, bus transfers).
  - Domestic & International leisure trip catalog (coastal resorts, city breaks).
  - Airline ticketing and custom visa/hotel inquiry flow.
  - Interactive day-by-day itinerary explorer with visual breakdown.
  - Floating and persistent direct WhatsApp & hotline contact triggers.
  - Ministry licensing and trust verification badge banner.
- **Constraints**:
  - No complex mandatory account creation or checkout barriers; conversion funnel leads directly to assisted consultation.
  - Must remain fast-loading on mobile cellular networks.

## Product Principles

1. **Uncompromising Trust First**: Official licensing, physical branch address, and 50-year heritage are displayed with prominence so visitors feel 100% secure.
2. **Clarity Over Clutter**: Package details (flight dates, hotel tiers, distance to holy sites, pricing) must be scannable in seconds without ambiguous hidden terms.
3. **Frictionless Human Connection**: The fastest path from interest to confirmed seat is a one-tap WhatsApp chat with pre-populated package context.
4. **Spiritual Dignity & Warm Hospitality**: The visual tone balances sacred reverence for Hajj/Umrah with uplifting warmth for vacation travel.
